Better Chat 1.0.0-test35f - Emoji-Only Message Sizing

- Detects messages that contain Unicode emoji (and whitespace) only.
- Enlarges emoji-only chat bubbles for a more natural Messenger-style presentation.
- Normal text + emoji messages keep the standard message size.
- Attachments, video, images, system messages, and normal BBC-rendered content are unaffected.
- Supports joined emoji, skin tones, flags, keycaps, and the symbol ranges used by Better Chat's emoji picker.
